OUR MISSION

Built for Students, by a Student

CU Hub was born from a simple frustration — CURAJ students had no central, modern platform to connect with each other, share resources, or solve everyday campus problems. Most solutions were scattered across WhatsApp groups, notice boards, and word-of-mouth, making it difficult for students to find what they needed quickly.

So CU Hub was built — a full-stack platform designed specifically for the CURAJ community. It brings together essential student needs in one place: buying and selling books, sharing study materials, reporting and finding lost items, and discovering campus events.

Built with a focus on reliability, privacy, and a smooth experience for every student.
